	\begin{enumerate}
		\item What are the mass and power limits for the system?

		\item Is the antenna intended to deploy only once or multiple times?

	\subsection{Identified requirements (STAND: 08.05)}
	\begin{itemize}
		\item The stowed antenna assembly shall fit within a volume of 1.5U $(10 \times 10 \times 15\,\mathrm{cm^3})$.
		
		\item The satellite system shall be designed to operate under space environmental conditions (vacuum, thermal variations, radiation).
		
		\item The antenna shall be deployable from the stowed configuration and retractable back into the satellite structure.
		
		\item The deployed antenna shall achieve a diameter of approximately 50 cm.
		
		\item The antenna shall deploy reliably and remain stable in its deployed position.
		
		\item The mechanism shall support multiple deployment and retraction cycles.
		
		\item The design shall be compatible with the FlatSat platform and its interface constraints.
		
		\item The mechanical system shall be simplified and suitable for multibody simulation.
		
		\item A locking or self-locking mechanism shall be implemented to prevent unintended movement.
		
		\item The overall system mass shall be minimized while maintaining functionality.
		
		\item The required motor torque and spring forces shall be estimated and dimensioned accordingly.
		
		\item Simplifications (e.g., bearings or small components) are permitted if they do not affect simulation results.
		
		\item The CAD model shall reflect realistic dimensions and approximate mass and inertia properties.
		
		\item Mechanical and electrical interfaces to the satellite platform shall be clearly defined.
		
		\item The design shall consider launch conditions such as vibration and mechanical loads.
		
		\item Safety aspects shall include failure cases such as incomplete deployment, blocked mechanisms, or failed retraction.
	\end{itemize}
